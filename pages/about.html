About HalValla

General

In my life, I've delved into a lot of different things. From mechanics to language, game design to coding, rubik's cubes to music composition, I've pretty much done what any man would deem "a lot". This website serves as a collection point for all my stuff that exists online - at least the stuff I deem worthy of sharing. Linked in the nav section there is (or should be) a linktree redirect that shows it all.

Music

This is probably the biggest bit, since it's the thing I've been doing pretty much all my life. I've always had a fascination and admiration for music. It really took off when I started playing piano in 2013, thereafter in 2017 when I started composing my own music (ragtime at the time), and I quickly got slapped onto the digital realm of music production. Many doors opened, I now had a brand new way of making music, and the sky was no longer the limit. Since that time I'm composed a handful of electronic pieces, orchestral pieces, and I'm currently working as a composer for Epictale Interactive on the game The Dreamthief

Game dev stuff

I recently (june 2025) wrapped up a higher professional education within the field of 3D art and game technology at Noroff Vocational. My interest for 3D and game design arose in...I think it was 2018 when I first downloaded Blender. It was a brutal start, since this was before the Blender facelift (2.8, I had 2.7x) My interest and curiosity within digital creation only grew from here. From small hobby-projects with my brother to doing my own one-off implementations, I started to gradually fall more and more in love with the world of game dev and digital art creation (not to mention music making was still high up)
